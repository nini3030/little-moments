Welcome to the studio! Strike a pose, make a memory ♡

Little Moments is a virtual photobooth that lives in your browser. Take four snaps with a countdown and flash, pick a filter and layout, decorate your strip, then download it like a real printed keepsake. Your photos never leave your device.

💕😘🦋
✨🥳🌷🍓

How it works:

1 · Snap 📷

Tap “Allow camera”, then “Take 4 photos”. Pose at every 3-2-1 countdown.

2 · Style 🎨

Choose a layout, filter and theme. Add stickers and a caption.

3 · Print 🖨️

Tap “Preview”, then download or share your strip.

About Little Moments

Purpose
Little Moments brings the fun of a classic photobooth online, so anyone can make a keepsake photo strip without leaving home.
How it works
  1. Allow camera access and press “Take 4 photos”.
  2. Choose a layout, filter, theme, stickers and caption.
  3. Preview your printed strip, then download or share it.
